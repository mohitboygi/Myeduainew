1. Parties and service

MyEdu AI is operated by Mohit Yadav under the brand Mohit Corporation. Mohit Corporation is not a registered company, LLP or other registered entity. The institution is the school or tuition class that signs up for MyEdu AI.

2. Institution responsibilities

Appoint authorised admins, keep logins secure, enter accurate data, and make sure it has the right to share student and parent data.

3. Accounts and access

The institution manages teacher, student and parent accounts. Teachers see only their classes. Parents see only their linked students.

4. Data security and processing

We keep institution data separate and secure and process student data only under the DPA.

5. Trial and payment

7-day free trial, then ₹50 per student per month (configured price). Payment is manual and access is activated after verification. No automatic deductions.
