Privacy Policy

2. Information we collect

  • Account: name, login ID, phone number or email, role (admin, teacher, student, parent), institution name, class.
  • Student and learning records: attendance, homework, tests, MCQ practice results, learning activity.
  • Parent and teacher details: name, contact details, linked class or student.
  • Technical: browser type, IP address, language setting and a login session token.

We do not collect card or UPI credentials in the app. Subscription payments are manual and verified by us.

3. Why we use it

To run the service for your institution, show attendance and exam records, keep accounts secure, verify payments, give support, meet legal duties and improve the product using aggregated data. We do not use children's data for targeted advertising.

4. Role of schools and tuition classes

For student records, the institution decides what is collected and why, and MyEdu AI processes it on the institution's instructions (see the Data Processing Agreement). For our own accounts, billing, security and consent records, MyEdu AI decides the purpose itself. Institutions must have the right to share their students' data with us.

5. Third-party technology providers

Cloudflare, Turso, Supabase and Neon have been identified as technology providers. Exactly which provider stores which data is being verified: [pending confirmation]. We will not claim that every provider holds every type of data. The web app loads open-source libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr, and some lab pages load Google Fonts, so those providers can see your IP address. We do not sell personal data.

6. Storage and security

We use role-based access, institution-level data separation, session tokens and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so please keep passwords private.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep data while the institution's account is active. After cancellation, data is kept for [30 days: to be confirmed] so the institution can export it, then deleted or anonymised unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, and make a complaint. Use the Privacy Requests page. Institution-managed student data is usually handled through the institution.

9. Withdrawing consent

Marketing consent can be withdrawn at any time on the Marketing Consent page. Withdrawing consent needed to run your account may mean we can no longer provide the service.

10. Personal data breach

If a breach affects your data we will investigate, inform the institution and affected people, and notify authorities where the law requires.

11. Children's privacy

See the Child Safety Policy. Students are under 18 in most cases, and their accounts are managed by their institution.

12. Marketing

Promotions from other Mohit Corporation businesses are optional, separate from the service, and sent only to parents and teachers who opt in. Student data is never shared for marketing.

13. International transfers

Some providers may store data outside India [server regions: to be verified]. Transfers will follow applicable law.

14. Legal status

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 and are being phased in. Consent manager provisions start on 13 November 2026, and most duties (notice, consent, children's data, rights, breach reporting) start on 13 May 2027. Until then the IT Act, 2000 and its rules apply. We are preparing for these duties now. Publishing this policy does not by itself make the platform legally compliant.
