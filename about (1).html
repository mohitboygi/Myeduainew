MyEdu AI

MyEdu AI is a school and tuition management web app for India. It covers attendance, announcements, MCQ practice, study PDFs and interactive 3D science labs, with separate logins for admins, teachers, students and parents, in English, Hindi and Gujarati.

The founder

Mohit Yadav is the founder and lead developer of MyEdu AI. He designs and builds the product and runs it under the brand Mohit Corporation, which also includes MOHORA and Mohit EV. He is based in Gujarat, India.
