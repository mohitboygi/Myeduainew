Draft v0.1 (22 Nov 2026), pending review by a qualified Indian lawyer. Items in [square brackets] are not final.

What the app actually uses

At the time of writing, the MyEdu AI web app does not set any browser cookies of its own. It uses your browser's local storage:

  • myedu_token: keeps you logged in (essential, authentication). Removed when you log out.
  • myedu_lang: remembers your language (functional).
  • myedu_cookie_pref and myedu_anon: store your cookie choice and a random ID for it (essential for honouring your choice).

These stay until you log out or clear your browser data.

Analytics and advertising

No analytics or advertising cookies or trackers are configured now. If we add any, they will stay off until you accept them.

Third parties

Libraries load from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr, and some lab pages load Google Fonts. These providers may log your IP address. We do not control their logs.

Your choices

Use the cookie banner or the Cookie settings link in the footer. You can change or withdraw non-essential choices at any time.
