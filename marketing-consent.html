Marketing & Communication Consent Policy

Draft v0.1 (22 Nov 2026), pending review by a qualified Indian lawyer. Items in [square brackets] are not final.

What this is

Optional promotions from MOHORA, Mohit EV and other Mohit Corporation businesses. Only for parents and teachers. This is separate from your educational service, and saying no never affects your access to MyEdu AI.

Your choices

Choose each business and each channel (email, WhatsApp, SMS, phone calls) separately. Everything is off by default.

What we do not do

  • We do not share MyEdu AI user data with other businesses automatically.
  • We do not use student data or contact details supplied by a school for independent marketing without proper authority and your consent.
  • We do not market to children or use children's data for targeted advertising.

Withdrawal

Withdraw at any time below. After withdrawal your contact is added to a suppression list so no further promotions are sent.

Rules for SMS and calls

Commercial SMS and calls must follow TRAI's rules (such as sender registration, consent records and Do Not Disturb preferences). We will check these before sending any.

To submit or manage this online, use the form in the app: https://myedu.pntr.dev/#/marketing-consent.
