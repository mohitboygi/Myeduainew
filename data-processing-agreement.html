Status

A school-facing template for review by your lawyer. It is not final.

1. Roles

For student, teacher and parent records entered by the institution, the institution is the Data Fiduciary and MyEdu AI is its Data Processor. For our own accounts, billing, security logs, consent records and website, MyEdu AI is an independent Data Fiduciary. Roles for any other activity must be assessed case by case.

2. Instructions and purpose

We process data only to provide the service and as the institution documents or instructs.

3. Data and people

Data: names, contact details, class, attendance, homework, tests, MCQ results. People: students, parents, teachers, institution admins.

4. Security

Role-based access, institution-level separation, encrypted connections, access logs and need-to-know staff access.

5. Sub-processors

Cloudflare, Turso, Supabase and Neon [exact role of each: to be confirmed]. We will update the list and flow down equivalent obligations.

6. Breach

We will tell the institution without undue delay [target: within 72 hours of confirming a breach] and help it meet its legal duties.

7. Data subject requests

We will help the institution answer access, correction and deletion requests.

8. Retention and deletion

Return or delete data when the agreement ends, as in the Institution Agreement.

9. Audit and cooperation

We will give reasonable written information and cooperate with reasonable audits.
